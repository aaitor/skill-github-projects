# Code of conduct

Treat people with respect, assume good intent, and keep feedback focused on the work. Welcome newcomers, explain technical disagreements clearly, and respect privacy and boundaries.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, and sustained disruption are unacceptable. These expectations apply to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and related project spaces.

Report conduct concerns privately to **aaitor@gmail.com**. Include relevant links and enough context to investigate; avoid posting sensitive details publicly. The maintainer will review reports with care and may remove content, restrict participation, or ban participants depending on severity and recurrence. Reports will be handled as privately as practical.

Maintainer: [Aitor Argomaniz](https://github.com/aaitor). For concerns involving the maintainer, GitHub's platform reporting tools remain available.
