# Embedded font notices

The shared banner and social card embed Instrument Serif, Geist, and Geist Mono under SIL Open Font License 1.1. Their font notices are embedded in the SVGs and included here:

- [Instrument Serif](licenses/Instrument-Serif-OFL.txt)
- [Geist and Geist Mono](licenses/Geist-OFL.txt)

The banner kit comes from [aaitor/assets](https://github.com/aaitor/assets). See [branding instructions](../../docs/branding.md) and `shared-assets.json` for source revision and generation commands. The repository's existing MIT license applies to its original code and documentation.
